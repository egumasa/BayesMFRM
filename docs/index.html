            Technical details: double-centering, local shrinkage & tie-invariant BFDR
            

              Interaction matrices are double-centered (B = P1 U P2) so rows and columns sum to zero and estimates are invariant to facet label ordering. Because double-centering projects isolated latent deviations slightly onto other cells, always evaluate bias against a practical threshold δ > 0 rather than strict zero. BFDR assigns identical scores to tied posterior error probabilities so ranking order never changes a decision.
            

          

        

      

    


    
    

      

        
From Diagnostics to Defensible Claims

        
4-Stage Diagnostic Triage & Interpretation Guardrails

        

          Follow this priority order before reporting substantive estimates. Never jump past an unresolved earlier stage.
        

        

          

            
Stage 1 • First

            
Design Identification

            
Check fit$design and likelihood_identified. Connectivity alone is insufficient.

            

              Guardrail: MCMC convergence NEVER fixes a rank-deficient design.
            

          

          

            
Stage 2 • Second

            
MCMC Stability

            
Verify R-hat < 1.01, bulk/tail ESS ≥ 400, 0 divergences, E-BFMI ≥ 0.30, and finite MCSE.

            

              Guardrail: Convergence means the sampler worked—not that the model fits your data.
            

          

          

            
Stage 3 • Third

            
Data Fit & PPC

            
Inspect overall and rater-level bayesplot::pp_check(..., group = "rater"), facet_fit(), and residual_cor_criteria() across competing models.

            

              Guardrail: Report remaining misfit transparently (like db08 above) rather than claiming good fit from overall averages alone.
            

          

          

            
Stage 4 • Fourth

            
Effects & Sensitivity

            
Separate pure rater adjustment from shrinkage; evaluate bias at practical δ > 0 with BFDR and alternative priors.

            

              Guardrail: A bias flag informs rater review/training—it is not an automatic mandate to delete a rater.
